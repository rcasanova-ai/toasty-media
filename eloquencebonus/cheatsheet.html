The Eloquence Cheat Sheet

1. The Daily Vocal Warm-Up (3 Minutes)

  • The Wall Drill (30s): Stand against a wall (heels, glutes, head touching). Speak your first sentence.
  • The Power Pitch (60s): Hum as low as you can, move up until your chest vibrates.
  • The T&D Snap (30s): "The distinct talent of the debt collector was documented today."
  • The Reset (30s): Perform 3 physiological sighs.

2. The "Executive" Lexicon

Stop Saying Start Saying Effect
I think we should... The most effective path is... Authority
Does that make sense? What are your thoughts? Confidence
I just wanted to check... I am following up on... High Status
I'm sorry for the wait. Thank you for your patience. Pivot to Power
It's kind of a problem. This is a significant bottleneck. Precision

3. Body Language Strike Zone

  • The Box: Keep all hand gestures between your waist and your chin.
  • Stillness = Status: When not gesturing, keep hands and head still.
  • The Down-Step: End sentences on a lower tone.

4. The Pause Protocol

  • Transitional (3s): Use between topics.
  • The Lip Lock: Touch lips together when you finish a thought.
  • The 1-Second Rule: Pause before responding.

5. Conflict & Boundary Scripts

De-Escalation
Lower volume 20%. Use your chest voice.

The Clean No
"I'm not able to take that on right now."

The Redirect
"I hear your concern about X, and that is exactly why we are focusing on Y."

6. The 60-Second Hook Structure

  • The Action: Start in the middle of a specific moment.
  • The Because: State your why early.
  • The Trio: Group your evidence into exactly three points.
  • The Command: Exit with a clear instruction.

The 10-Second Pre-Meeting Checklist

  • Shoulders: Are they down and back?
  • Breath: Is it in my belly?
  • Hands: Are they visible?
  • First Word: Do I know exactly what my first sentence is?
