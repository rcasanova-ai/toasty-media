THE ELOQUENCE

5-DAY EXECUTIVE PROGRAM

Day 1: Diction & Syntax (The Mechanics)

Goal: Purge lazy language and master the rhythm of authority.

  • The Lazy Word Purge: Identify 3 "lazy" adjectives (e.g., good, bad, think) you use most often. Commit to their Executive Alternatives (Exemplary, Suboptimal, Maintain).
  • The Short Punch: Practice ending a long explanation with a 3-5 word anchor sentence.
  • Synonym Swap: Rewrite one "default" sentence from your upcoming meeting into a "Boardroom" context.

Day 2: Articulation & Physics

Goal: Transform muffled speech into sharp, professional delivery.

  • Terminal Consonant Check: Consciously "click" the last letter of every sentence (T, K, P, D).
  • Vertical Space: Practice dropping your jaw vertically on "O" and "A" sounds to increase resonance.
  • The Down-Swing: Record a 30-second clip and ensure your pitch drops at the end of declarative statements.

Day 3: Vocal Mastery & Resonance

Goal: Move the source of your voice from the throat to the diaphragm.

  • Shoulder Check: Stand in front of a mirror; ensure your shoulders remain still during deep inhales.
  • Core Engagement: Perform 10 "Ha!" pulses to engage the diaphragm before your first call.
  • The Power Note: Find your resonance by humming until you feel vibration in your chest.

Day 4: Physical Eloquence & Stillness

Goal: Align your body language with your verbal authority.

  • The Navel Rule: Ensure your torso is pointed directly at your primary listener.
  • The Strike Zone: Keep all hand gestures between your waist and chin; avoid "hidden hands."
  • Stillness Test: Practice speaking for 2 minutes without shifting weight or tilting your head.

Day 5: The Art of the Pause

Goal: Use silence to command the room and eliminate fillers.

  • The Mouth Lock: Physically touch your lips together the moment you finish a sentence to prevent "um/uh."
  • The Emphatic Beat: Count to one (silently) before delivering your most important word or data point.
  • Zero-Sound Transitions: Replace "So" and "And" with a 2-second reflective pause.
