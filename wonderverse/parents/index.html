Safety & privacy

This first version is a scripted, browser-based experience without freeform AI chat, child accounts, uploads, or paid subscriptions. It does not collect a child's name, age, photo, voice, or email. Progress is stored only in this browser using local storage. Parents can erase it above or in the adventure.

Wonderverse is designed for shared discovery. Adults should supervise young children and guide any activities outside the browser.
