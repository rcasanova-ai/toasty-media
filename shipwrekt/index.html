Human & operational risk intelligence

Even a seaworthy ship can have a reckless crew.

Security failures can begin with everyday decisions. With your authorization, SHIPWREKT examines repository activity, collaborator permissions, audit events, deployment history and operating procedures to find preventable risks before they become incidents. Findings are based on observable evidence, not assumptions about people's intentions.

Production Scallywags

Spot direct-to-production changes, bypassed reviews, failed or skipped checks, and unusually short commit-to-deploy intervals. Fast releases are assessed against the safeguards in place, not automatically treated as failures.

Ghosts on the Crew List

Flag dormant collaborators, lingering administrator privileges, stale access tokens and accounts that may no longer need access. Departure status must be confirmed by the organization.

Rules of the Ship

Compare written SOPs with real audit and deployment events. Identify missing approvals, unclear ownership, untested recovery procedures and repeated exceptions, then recommend practical controls.

Available through scoped repository and operational reviews. Access is read-only where supported, limited to agreed systems and time periods, and findings include evidence, context and corrective actions. We do not secretly monitor employees or infer character from activity logs.

Questions

Before we come aboard.

Is SHIPWREKT only for AI-built apps?

No. AI-assisted and vibe-coded products are a focus, but the same security fundamentals apply to traditionally built software.

Do you need our passwords?

No passwords by email. We agree on safe, least-privilege access or temporary test accounts when authenticated assessment is required.

Does a passing assessment mean we're secure?

No assessment guarantees security. We report what was tested, what was found and what remained outside scope.

Will you fix every issue for $1,000?

No. The package includes a scoped assessment, report and one hour with a qualified developer. Implementation and retesting are separately scoped.
