Inside Stories with Wings

What happens when stories lose their wings?

Stories with Wings uses a world of birds to explore censorship, surveillance, authoritarian control, and the destruction of knowledge. Some birds are caged. One echoes the visual language of The Handmaid's Tale. Another is metallic, red-eyed, and Orwellian. The crows do not burn the tree. They cut away its branches until the knowledge it carries begins to disappear.

The metaphor is simple: stories need freedom to move. When people start deciding which stories are allowed to fly, the damage reaches far beyond a single book.
