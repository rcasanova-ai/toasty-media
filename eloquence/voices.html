Eloquence Protocol · Voice Lab

Find your practice voice.

Preview natural English voices for guided communication exercises. American and British voices are available. Your text stays on your device.

Instant device preview works without a download, but uses your phone’s built-in voice. Generate and listen downloads Kokoro’s smaller quantized model on first use and caches it where the browser allows.

Powered by Kokoro 82M, Apache 2.0 licensed. Voices are generated locally after the initial model download. No API account or subscription is required. First use downloads approximately 92 MB plus supporting files; later visits normally reuse the browser cache. Your browser may evict cached files. Lower-powered phones may take longer or be unable to run the model.
