How Dough works

One view. Two balances.

Spending budget is money you or your organization fund for recurring research and marketplace activity. Earnings are what your profile, Dub and Jams have generated. They are accounted for separately so Peeps does not pretend your earnings are a bank deposit.

Research budgets

Set it once. Let the agent work inside the limit.

Example: “Spend up to $20 screening this search. Never spend more than $3 on one person.” Peeps can reserve or authorize that budget with the payment provider and meter x402 interactions underneath without asking you to approve every penny.

Payment infrastructure

You use Dough. The rails stay underneath.

Peeps should use regulated payment providers for fiat funding and payouts and embedded wallet infrastructure for supported crypto settlement. No seed phrase, SOL balance or chain selection is required for ordinary users.
