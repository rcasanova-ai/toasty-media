Production · real services

Intent → human interaction → verified evidence

Everything below is read from the production backend. Each money line says whether it was on-chain (confirmed on Solana by the server), off-chain (Dough ledger), simulated or pending. Nothing here is seeded. You need a second inbox of your own to play the guest.

How to read this page: what each label means

Steps: 1 Find my Peeps → 2 Authorize introductions → the guest accepts on their private page and Peeps books the first mutual time → 3 Join Jam (needs camera/mic) → add the transcript → settle. Full click-by-click guide: Judge walkthrough ↗ · seeded fallback: /peeps/demo/.

  • ONCHAIN VERIFIED: a Solana transaction the server itself read from the chain and checked (devnet unless the network says otherwise). Shown with an Explorer link.
  • VERIFIED CONFIDENTIAL: a shielded Zcash payment the recipient's own wallet confirmed. The network is always shown; REGTEST is a local test network with no value.
  • OFFCHAIN: an internal Dough ledger entry. Never a blockchain payout.
  • SIMULATED: demo content, recorded-not-sent email, or a payment from the demo provider. No funds moved.
  • PENDING: the evidence doesn't exist yet. The reason is shown; nothing is invented to pass.
