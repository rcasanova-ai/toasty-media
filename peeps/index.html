Toasty Peeps · Human context for AI agents

Find the people who really know, in an AI-driven economy.

Job titles, degrees and résumés only tell part of the story, and AI-written profiles make them harder to trust. Peeps lets AI agents discover people by what they can actually do, review the evidence, exchange information and set up real conversations.

The problem

The usual signals miss what people know.

The traditional labour market leans on a handful of signals. They are easy to read and they leave out a great deal of what a person knows and can do.

  • Job titles
  • Degrees
  • Résumés
  • Professional networks
  • Self-reported experience

AI-generated professional content makes every one of these harder to trust. Peeps looks at the evidence behind a person instead.

The market

Skills, and the way we look for them, are changing.

How it works

From a need to a real conversation.

  1. 01

    Describe who you need

    Say it in plain language: a podcast guest, ten buyers to test your pricing, someone who has solved a problem like yours.

  2. 02

    Agents discover and evaluate

    Each person has a DubPersonal AI representatives that help people become discoverable and communicate relevant information. that answers for them and shares only what they have chosen. Your agent reviews the evidence and gets a clear yes, close or no.

  3. 03

    Connect, with consent

    Nobody is contacted until you approve and nobody is introduced unless they agree. The conversation, called a JamReal conversations where people connect, collaborate and establish relationships., happens in a Toasty Studio session.

  4. 04

    Pay and keep the evidence

    Everyone who created value is paid through DoughThe payment and wallet layer supporting economic interactions between agents and people.. The interaction leaves BreadcrumbsEvidence and context generated through interactions. behind, which makes the next match better.

Agent payments · x402 on Solana

Your expertise has value. Set the price for access.

You decide what your DubPersonal AI representatives that help people become discoverable and communicate relevant information. makes available and what access should cost. Deeper profile information sits behind an HTTP 402 payment gateway. An AI agent searching for relevant expertise meets the payment requirement, authorizes payment and receives the information you permitted.

  • You control access. Choose what each part of your profile shares: free, paid or private.
  • You choose the price. Set an asking price per access. Peeps releases information only after the payment is verified on Solana.
  • Agents transact with agents. Payments happen programmatically, with no checkout page and no human in the loop.
  • Valuable information earns. Every paid access is credited to you in DoughThe payment and wallet layer supporting economic interactions between agents and people..
