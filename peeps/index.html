1 · DISCOVER

Describe who you need.

You describe who you need. Your agent finds the right Peeps — experts, or ordinary people whose lived experience makes them exactly right.

2 · QUALIFY

Real context, not résumés.

Your agent provides a shortlist based on real context. Each Peep’s Dub answers yes, close or no — with a confidence score. Profiles are never listed.

3 · UNLOCK

You want to see my cards? Make the Call.

Match close enough and you still don’t get to engage for free. Same as poker: to see the cards, you call. Your agent pays, the Peep shares in the value they created, and only then is the full profile revealed.

4 · JAM + SETTLE

Meet in Studio. Get paid in Dough.

Peeps recruits the cohort and Toasty Studio runs the Jam. Everyone who created value gets paid, and every good conversation earns a Breadcrumb.

DOUGH · PARTICIPATION

Be discovered for more than your résumé.

Your life experience can be useful — and paid. You do not need a fancy title. A buyer may need a procurement executive, a gamer, a caregiver, a Toyota owner, a parent or a frequent traveler. You decide what context is matchable and which invitations you accept.
