Toasty Peeps · Human context, agent-accessible

The human layer for the agent economy.

AI agents can research, schedule and write. What they cannot do is have lived experience. Peeps connects agents — and the people who use them — to real humans: found by what they have actually done and known, reached only with their consent, and paid for their time.

How it works

From “who do I need?” to a finished conversation.

  1. 01

    Describe who you need

    A podcast guest, ten buyers to test your pricing, a former procurement lead for a diligence call. Say it in plain language.

  2. 02

    Discover and qualify

    Peeps looks for people whose real experience fits and shows you a shortlist, each with a clear yes, close or no and why.

  3. 03

    Introduce, with consent

    Nobody is contacted until you approve, and nobody is introduced unless they say yes. Their private details stay private.

  4. 04

    Hold the Jam

    The conversation happens in a Toasty Studio session — recorded, transcribed and produced.

  5. 05

    Settle and learn

    Everyone who created value is paid, and the outcome is recorded so the next match is better.

The five ideas

A few words, plainly.

Dub

Your private profile for matching. You choose which experiences can be matched and which stay private. A Dub answers a request with yes, close or no — and never reveals more than you approved.

Discovery

Finding people by what they have done, experienced and know — an engineer, a caregiver, a Toyota owner, a former CFO — not by job title alone.

Introduction

A consented first contact. Both sides must agree before anything is shared or scheduled, and either side can say no at any point.

Jam

The conversation itself: an interview, focus group, advisory call or panel, held in Toasty Studio with recording and transcript.

Dough

The value that moves through Peeps. Requesters pay to be introduced; the people who take part share in what they helped create.

Verifiable outcomes

Every completed Jam leaves a receipt — who took part, what happened, what was paid — that can be checked rather than just trusted.

For the people in the network

Be found for more than your résumé.

You do not need a fancy title. A buyer may need a procurement executive — or a gamer, a caregiver, a parent, a frequent traveller. You decide what context is matchable and which invitations you accept, and you are paid for the time you give.

  • Private by default. Questions, recordings, transcripts and findings stay inside the engagement.
  • Permissioned profile. Your Dub shares only what you have approved; everything else stays unknown.
