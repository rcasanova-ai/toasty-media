Before you apply

So who the hell is Ricardo?

I'm Ricardo Casanova. I'm a Canadian living in Bangkok, a founder and product guy, and someone who's been around technology long enough to remember when getting a computer to do anything useful felt like a personal victory. I've built businesses, worked with people across different countries and industries, and collected more than a few stories along the way. Some are funny. Some are absurd. A few are the kind of thing you wouldn't believe if I hadn't been there.

My most important qualification? I'm fucking hilarious. At least I think so. You can be the judge. I love a good argument, a ridiculous tangent and someone who can make me laugh when I'm taking myself too seriously. I have opinions, but I also love having them challenged. Especially by someone who's actually thought about what they're saying.

Why start this podcast?

Because I miss conversations that go somewhere unexpected. The ones where you start talking about a new AI product and somehow end up discussing human nature, terrible business decisions, cultural misunderstandings or a completely unrelated disaster from twenty years ago. I don't want to spend an hour reading headlines at someone. I want to have the sort of conversation people wish they were sitting in on.

What would we talk about?

Technology and AI, the strange behavior of people, business and startups, life in different countries, culture, relationships, current events, personal screwups, bizarre experiences and whatever else deserves a proper conversation. We'll have guests, sometimes get serious, sometimes disagree, and occasionally go so far off topic that we have to figure out how we got there. The subject matters, but the chemistry matters more.

What's the humor like?

Quick, sarcastic, irreverent and occasionally dark. I appreciate self-deprecation, sharp observations, terrible timing and people who can take a joke and fire one back. Nothing needs to be relentlessly edgy, and I don't want cruelty masquerading as comedy. We should be able to laugh at ourselves, ask awkward questions, challenge nonsense and know when a story deserves an honest moment instead of a punchline.

Why would you want to do this with me?

You'll be helping shape a show from its first episode, not squeezing yourself into a format someone else already perfected. We'll experiment, meet interesting people, chase strange ideas and make something we would actually want to watch. I'm building Toasty Studio too, so we get to make the show using the tools we're creating. You don't need to agree with me or admire me. I'd much rather find someone who is curious, dependable, comfortable being themselves and capable of telling me when I'm full of shit.

The job description: Be interesting. Be interested. Show up. Have fun. And when I say something ridiculous, please don't just nod along.

Video audition guidelines

Keep it to about two minutes. Tell us who you are, what you have strong opinions about, and why you'd be fun to have on the show. We may throw you a surprise question in the live round.

Keep your real background visible, with no blur or virtual background. Wear whatever you want. No suits, costumes or fancy equipment required. Find somewhere reasonably quiet, face the light, and use your normal voice. Your surroundings and your personality are part of the introduction. Please make sure nobody else is visible or audible without their permission.

Before recording: Studio invitations are sent individually after review. Please do not send recordings through public links. The audition is free; there is no guarantee of selection or payment.

Recording and clip release

Auditions may become part of the show, trailers, promotional clips or behind-the-scenes content, including those of applicants who are not selected. You control whether to apply and must accept the release below before submitting. The release covers submitted auditions and any follow-up recordings that you separately agree to participate in. We will not use your personal contact details for publicity. Applicants must be 18 or older.
