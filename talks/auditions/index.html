Video audition guidelines

Keep it to about two minutes. Tell us who you are, what you have strong opinions about, and why you'd be fun to have on the show. We may throw you a surprise question in the live round.

Keep your real background visible, with no blur or virtual background. Wear whatever you want. No suits, costumes or fancy equipment required. Find somewhere reasonably quiet, face the light, and use your normal voice. Your surroundings and your personality are part of the introduction. Please make sure nobody else is visible or audible without their permission.

Before recording: Studio invitations are sent individually after review. Please do not send recordings through public links. The audition is free; there is no guarantee of selection or payment.

Recording and clip release

Auditions may become part of the show, trailers, promotional clips or behind-the-scenes content, including those of applicants who are not selected. You control whether to apply and must accept the release below before submitting. The release covers submitted auditions and any follow-up recordings that you separately agree to participate in. We will not use your personal contact details for publicity. Applicants must be 18 or older.
