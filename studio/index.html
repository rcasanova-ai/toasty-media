Run the room

Guests, graphics and sound — in one control surface.

Bring people in with a link, then run the show from a single, stable set of controls. Nothing moves while you are recording, presenting or live.

  • Guest management. Invite by link, see who is connected, mute or remove people, and choose who is on screen.
  • Layouts and lower thirds. Switch between speaker, grid and spotlight layouts. Names and titles appear as broadcast-style lower thirds.
  • Soundboard and ticker. Play sound effects into the program and run a text ticker without leaving the page.
  • Run of show. Plan topics and timing, move through them as you go, and keep the show on schedule.

Present

Share a screen and the program follows.

Press Present, choose a screen, window or browser tab, and it fills the stage with your camera in a picture-in-picture corner. The program — and anything recording or streaming it — updates by itself.

  • One control. Present is the only screen-sharing button. Stop presenting and you are back to your camera.
  • Slides, demos, documents. Anything you can put on a screen can go in the program.
  • Name always visible. Your lower third stays readable on the picture-in-picture camera.

An AI production partner, not another tool to manage.

Moxie listens to the session, answers you privately, and does the legwork a producer would — while you stay in the conversation. It suggests; you decide what the audience sees.

Moxie works with your own AI provider key, so you control the model and the cost. Everything it proposes for the program waits for your approval.

Recording & transcription

Your recording is made on your machine.

Studio records the finished program in your browser at 6 Mbps video — straight to an MP4 where your browser supports it. Nothing has to be uploaded to get your file.

  • Download immediately. Stop recording and the MP4 is ready on your device.
  • Crash-safe. The recording is saved in small pieces while you record. If your tab closes or your laptop dies, the footage is recovered next time you open Studio.
  • Live transcript. Captured as you talk, with TXT and JSON export.
  • Optional conversion. Browsers that can only record WebM can have it converted to MP4 on Toasty’s servers, within plan limits.

Live streaming

Go live to every channel at once.

Connect X, YouTube, TikTok or Instagram once with each platform’s stream URL and key. After that, Go Live is a toggle and a button.

  • One program, many destinations. The same picture and sound go to every channel; if one fails, the others keep going.
  • Automatic recording. Going live starts a recording, so you always have the show.
  • Keys stay private. Stream keys are stored encrypted on the server and never shown again.

Each platform decides who can stream to it; some require a minimum following or approval.
