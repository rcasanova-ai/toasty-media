Session Planner

Walk in with a plan, and keep it in front of you.

Great sessions are prepared before anyone presses record. Session Planner guides you from the first idea to a finished plan, collects what your guests want you to know and brings all of it into the studio with you.

  • Start from the kind of show you make. Interview, podcast, panel, book club, livestream or a blank custom session, each with a sensible starting run of show.
  • Prepare your guests. Each guest receives a private invitation by email, with a briefing you have reviewed, and fills in their own preparation page. They need no account.
  • Know what they want. Introductions, expertise, topics they would like to discuss, topics to avoid, pronunciation and links arrive in one place, visible only to you and your producer.
  • Keep it close while you record. Your topics, opening questions, talking points and each guest’s preparation sit in the studio beside your run of show.
  • Book clubs without the homework. The book club template builds a short briefing on the premise, characters and themes, so guests can join without having read the whole book.

Moxie · In the beta

Meet Moxie. Your AI producer.

Every great show needs a great producer. Moxie listens to your session, follows the conversation and responds when you say her name. Say “Hey Moxie” and ask for what you need while you are recording.

Forgotten a name? Want the exact thing a guest said twenty minutes ago? About to mention someone you would like to know more about? Ask Moxie and keep talking. Her answers appear privately on your screen, and nothing reaches your guests or your audience until you approve it.

Listens for her name

Say “Hey Moxie” whenever you need her. The rest of the conversation is left alone.

Follows the conversation

Moxie keeps up with what is being said, so “what did our guest say about the acquisition?” has an answer.

Knows your plan

She reads your session plan and your guests’ preparation, so she knows who is joining and what they want to discuss.

Finds what you need

Background on a person, company or topic, prepared as a card with its source.

Shares only when you say so

Results stay with you. Say “yes, share that with everyone” or press a button, and Moxie posts it to the session chat with the source link.

Puts it on screen

Approve an article or image and it takes over the program. Say “take it off” and your layout returns.

Keeps the show moving

Moxie follows your run of show and tells you when a segment is nearly out of time.

RTR · Read The Room Coming soon

Read The Room. Catch the moments that matter.

The best moments in a conversation are easy to miss when you are busy running it. RTR is an intelligent production capability that watches the room alongside you, so a great exchange is marked while it is happening and easy to find when you are ready to clip it.

RTR is designed to notice the way a conversation feels, and to help the producer flag the moments worth keeping. Highlights, memorable exchanges and potential viral clips are identified as they unfold, which gives you the foundation for faster editing and sharper clips.

What RTR will pay attention to

  • Body language and facial reactionsHow guests respond to what is being said
  • Vocal tone and conversational energyWhen the pace, volume or intensity of a conversation shifts
  • Laughter and emotional reactionsThe moments that make an audience lean in
  • Awkward and unexpected momentsThe exchanges people remember and talk about
  • Clip potentialMoments you can mark now and cut later
