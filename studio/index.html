Session Planner

Walk in with a plan, and keep it in front of you.

Great sessions are prepared before anyone presses record. Session Planner is where you set up the production, shape the conversation and get your guests ready. When you open the studio, the plan comes with you.

  • Plan the session. Choose the format, from podcast and interview to panel, webinar and workshop, then set the date, the length, the host and the producer.
  • Prepare your guests. Add your speakers, collect what you need from them and see at a glance who is ready.
  • Structure the conversation. Build a run of show with segments, timings and notes. Inside the studio, give each topic its talking points and prepared questions, and move through them as the conversation unfolds.
  • Shape the audience experience. Set up an event page, replay, audience chat, Q&A and polls, and add your sponsors.
  • Check readiness. A final checklist confirms that your host, producer, branding and recording setup are ready before you go.

Moxie · In the beta

Meet Moxie. Your AI producer.

Every great show needs a great producer. Moxie follows the conversation, takes your instructions out loud or by text, and finds what you need while you are recording.

Lost a name? Want the exact thing a guest said twenty minutes ago? About to mention someone you would like to know more about? Ask Moxie and keep talking. The answer appears privately on your screen, and nothing reaches your audience until you decide it should.

Listens and follows the session

Moxie keeps up with the conversation as it happens, so it understands what is being discussed when you ask.

Takes instructions the way you give them

Hold to talk or type a request mid-conversation. Moxie answers without pulling you out of the moment.

Remembers what was said

Ask what a guest said earlier and Moxie brings back the moment in their own words.

Finds the information you need

Ask for background on a person, company or topic. Moxie prepares it as a card you can put on screen with one click.

Keeps the show moving

Moxie follows your run of show, tells you when a segment is nearly out of time and keeps what is coming next in view.

Leaves you in charge

Everything Moxie prepares is private to you until you approve it for the program.

RTR · Read The Room Coming soon

Read The Room. Catch the moments that matter.

The best moments in a conversation are easy to miss when you are busy running it. RTR is an intelligent production capability that watches the room alongside you, so a great exchange is marked while it is happening and easy to find when you are ready to clip it.

RTR is designed to notice the way a conversation feels, and to help the producer flag the moments worth keeping. Highlights, memorable exchanges and potential viral clips are identified as they unfold, which gives you the foundation for faster editing and sharper clips.

What RTR will pay attention to

  • Body language and facial reactionsHow guests respond to what is being said
  • Vocal tone and conversational energyWhen the pace, volume or intensity of a conversation shifts
  • Laughter and emotional reactionsThe moments that make an audience lean in
  • Awkward and unexpected momentsThe exchanges people remember and talk about
  • Clip potentialMoments you can mark now and cut later

The production

One room for the whole show.

Studio is an integrated production environment. Everything you need to run a professional recording or broadcast sits in the same place, and the controls stay where you left them.

Recording

Record the program in your browser at high quality and download the finished MP4 the moment you stop. Your recording is saved continuously while you work.

Live production

Go live to X, YouTube, TikTok and Instagram from the same room. Going live records the show too, so you always have a copy.

Guest invitations

Send a link and guests join from their browser. See who is connected, mute or remove people and choose who appears on screen.

Producer controls

Switch layouts, bring up lower thirds, play from the soundboard, run a ticker and cut to scenes such as Starting Soon and Be Right Back.

Camera and microphone

Preview your camera and microphone before you join, then manage them from a control bar that stays in the same place throughout the session.

Screen sharing

Present a screen, window or browser tab with your camera in the corner. Slides, demos and documents go straight into the program.

Program output

A clean output window shows exactly what your audience sees, ready for recording or streaming.

Recordings and transcripts

Download your recording when the session ends, along with a transcript in text or JSON.

Your brand throughout

Your logo, colours and lower thirds carry through the room, the live program and the finished recording.
