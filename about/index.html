Background

Experience across mobile, telecom and AI.

Ricardo Casanova is a technology entrepreneur, AI consultant and thought leader working across artificial intelligence, human intelligence and emerging technologies.

His work currently focuses on three ventures: 8ALTA, providing AI consulting, strategy and implementation; Santati, developing a human behavioral intelligence layer for businesses and intelligent systems; and Toasty Peeps, exploring how people and autonomous AI agents can work together in an increasingly automated economy.

His career spans telecommunications, mobile technology, IoT, machine-to-machine systems, big data and machine learning. He has worked for organizations serving government and has worked extensively with government directly, alongside large enterprises. He has advised on commercial strategy, partnerships and the adoption of emerging technologies.

Based between Thailand and Canada, Ricardo continues to develop applied research initiatives in embodied AI, intelligent infrastructure and human-machine interaction.

Recognition

Honoured for contribution to the community.

Ricardo received two commemorations of Queen Elizabeth II's Platinum Jubilee in 2022, one from the Province of Alberta and one from the Government of Canada.

Provincial medal

Queen Elizabeth II’s Platinum Jubilee Medal (Alberta)

Conferred by command of the Lieutenant Governor of Alberta in commemoration of the seventieth anniversary of Her Majesty’s accession to the Throne, and in recognition of valuable contribution to the province.

Certificate

Lieutenant Governor’s certificate

The medal was accompanied by a certificate from the Lieutenant Governor of Alberta, dated 2022, the year of the Platinum Jubilee.

Federal pin

Platinum Jubilee Pin of Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II

The commemorative pin and certificate presented through a Member of Parliament on behalf of the Government of Canada, recognizing community leadership.
