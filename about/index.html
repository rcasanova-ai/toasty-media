About

Ricardo Casanova, founder of Toasty Media.

Ricardo is a technology executive, entrepreneur, speaker and author with more than twenty years in enterprise IT services, telecommunications, managed services, emerging technologies and AI. He builds companies and products where technology meets the way people communicate.

Background

Experience across enterprise technology and AI.

20+ yearsEnterprise technology and business development
Canada + ThailandInternational experience and ventures
Strategy to deliveryTechnology, procurement and product building

Ricardo has spent more than two decades helping large enterprises navigate and adopt complex technologies. His background spans enterprise communications and collaboration, advanced networking and connectivity, managed IT services, industrial systems, machine-to-machine (M2M) communications, SCADA and machine learning (ML), technologies that laid important groundwork for modern AI. He brings together technical understanding, commercial strategy and business development to turn sophisticated solutions into real-world outcomes. His work also includes government procurement and RFP strategy, with a strong record of successful bids.

Working across Canada and Thailand, Ricardo now builds AI products, explores human behavior, develops businesses and creates original media. He founded Toasty Media, the company behind Toasty Studio and Toasty Peeps, while helping shape Santati's human-centered intelligence platform and advancing AI transformation through 8ALTA. His independent ventures, including Get Thai'd Down, extend that same curiosity into new markets and everyday problems. Across these projects, he is focused on turning ambitious ideas into products people can actually use.

Recognition

Honoured for contribution to the community.

Ricardo received two commemorations of Queen Elizabeth II's Platinum Jubilee in 2022, one from the Province of Alberta and one from the Government of Canada.

Provincial medal

Queen Elizabeth II’s Platinum Jubilee Medal (Alberta)

Conferred by command of the Lieutenant Governor of Alberta in commemoration of the seventieth anniversary of Her Majesty’s accession to the Throne, and in recognition of valuable contribution to the province.

Certificate

Lieutenant Governor’s certificate

The medal was accompanied by a certificate from the Lieutenant Governor of Alberta, dated 2022, the year of the Platinum Jubilee.

Federal pin

Platinum Jubilee Pin of Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II

The commemorative pin and certificate presented through a Member of Parliament on behalf of the Government of Canada, recognizing community leadership.

Soccer & documentary filmmaking

Community, culture and the beautiful game.

Ricardo founded YEG Soccer to advocate for better access to soccer in Edmonton. His work in the community led to The YEG Soccer Project, a documentary he created through the TELUS STORYHIVE program. The film explores soccer's cultural importance, community connections and the challenges facing grassroots participation.
