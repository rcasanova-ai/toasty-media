Background

Operating between Edmonton, the Bay Area and Bangkok.

Ricardo works across AI, mobile, IoT, agents and decentralized infrastructure, and with the communities and companies building them. That corridor gives him a practical view of where technology changes a business — and where it does not.

Toasty Media grew out of the same question that runs through his speaking and writing: how do real people get heard, and found, in a world increasingly mediated by software? Studio is the answer for conversations. Peeps is the answer for the people in them.
