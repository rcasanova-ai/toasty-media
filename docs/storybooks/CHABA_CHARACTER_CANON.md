# Chaba: Phyll and Wisp storybook character canon

**Mandatory for all Toasty Media Phyll and Wisp children's storybooks.** Any dog appearing in the stories or illustrations is **Chaba**, modeled on Ricardo's real dog shown in the two photos shared on October 31, 2026. Never substitute a generic dog or use a different name.

Chaba's adult appearance: fluffy reddish-brown and white coat; white blaze down face; dark eyes and nose; upright triangular ears; fluffy chest and neck; large bushy plume tail curling high over her back. Maintain recognizable markings and proportions in the books' illustration style. The puppy reference photo may be used for scenes depicting Chaba as a puppy.

Use the name **Chaba** consistently in manuscript text, illustration prompts, captions, character sheets, metadata and QA. Review existing dog scenes for compliance when editing existing books. The original adult and puppy reference photographs were provided in the ChatGPT conversation; obtain them as assets for production illustration rather than substituting stock images.
