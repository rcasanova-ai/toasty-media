# Toasty Human Insight Network

## Product thesis

Toasty is infrastructure for high-value human conversations. Peeps is the permissioned human participation network; Studio is the live conversation environment; Dubs are portable participant representations; Hottie helps prepare and run sessions; Dough handles compensation; Jams are durable engagement records.

Peeps is not limited to experts. A useful participant may be valuable because of professional expertise, lived experience, demographics, ownership, behavior, interests, geography, or a combination of those attributes.

## First-class session types

- Expert panel
- Focus group
- Research interview cohort
- Customer conversation
- Product / concept test
- Podcast / public conversation
- Advisory consultation

The buyer defines who needs to be in the room, why, cohort size and consent-safe matching criteria. Peeps recruits and screens. Studio runs the session.

## Dub lifecycle

1. A participant may enter a session without first creating a full Peeps profile.
2. Toasty may prepare a bounded Pre-Dub from participant-supplied or appropriately sourced information.
3. During the session, approved context can help Hottie support the moderator or host.
4. After the session, the participant can claim or enrich their Dub.
5. Reusable attributes only enter the participant profile according to participant permission.
6. The participant can export their Dub as JSON.
7. A participant can opt into future paid matching.

Dubs must not silently absorb a client's proprietary session findings, confidential questions, or private transcript content.

## Data-rights boundary

### Participant profile data
Owned/controlled by the participant. Includes approved identity, expertise, lived-context attributes, evidence, availability, participation preferences and consent settings.

### Client engagement data
Scoped to the paying engagement. Includes private brief, proprietary questions, recordings, transcript, client findings, deliverables and confidential engagement content.

### Platform demand data
Owned/operated by Toasty at the platform level when aggregated/de-identified. Includes requested cohort attributes, search/filter frequency, geography demand, time-to-fill, scarcity, invitation acceptance, incentive pressure and session-type demand. It must not expose the client identity or reconstruct confidential client strategy.

## Demand intelligence

Peeps should learn which human profiles are in demand without selling advertising or client findings. Examples:

- frequently requested cohort attributes
- emerging geography demand
- scarce combinations of context
- fill time by cohort
- incentive required to recruit a cohort
- session format demand
- participant supply versus buyer demand

Participants can use these signals to understand where their approved profile is relevant. Buyers get better matching and realistic recruiting expectations.

## Distribution loop

Host creates session → participants join → Dubs are started/enriched → participants claim Dubs → participants opt into future paid work → demand matching creates new invitations → some participants later host or request their own sessions → more people enter the network.

The signup proposition is not “make a profile.” It is “claim the useful, portable representation and opportunity history created from your participation.”

## Privacy principles

- Matchable does not mean public.
- Participation is opt-in.
- Client engagement output remains isolated from platform demand analytics.
- Sensitive demographic categories require explicit policy and consent controls.
- No advertising audience sale is part of this model.
- De-identified demand intelligence must meet aggregation thresholds before external exposure in production.
