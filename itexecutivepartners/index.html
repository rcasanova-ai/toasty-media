Our recruitment methodology

A recruitment process that goes beyond CVs.

We manage each recruitment assignment from the initial brief through candidate selection and appointment. Our process combines targeted executive search with structured assessment of professional capability, leadership experience, business understanding and suitability for the organisation. We do not accept every recruitment assignment. We take on engagements where our expertise, network and methodology provide the right foundation for finding the right candidate.

01 / DISCOVER

Define success

Work directly with the hiring company to define the responsibilities, organisational context and outcomes expected from the appointment.

02 / SEARCH & ASSESS

Evaluate the whole candidate

Identify and screen candidates through our selected specialist network, considering professional experience, leadership capability, business understanding and role suitability.

03 / SUPPORT

Follow through

Coordinate the recruitment through appointment, then support the transition with separate 90-day plans and structured follow-up.

Candidate assessment

Human dynamics matter in an AI-driven world.

Our recruitment methodology uses Santati Human Dynamics to assess executives, managers and specialists. Its dedicated AI module examines a candidate’s attitude towards AI, their approach to its practical application and their ability to adopt AI. These assessments add context beyond technical knowledge and CV experience. Our structured screening process also helps us clarify each mandate, explore relevant experience and identify candidates whose capabilities and career objectives align with the role. Technology supports this process, while our recruiters remain responsible for candidate evaluation and shortlisting.

SANTATI HUMAN DYNAMICS

AI readiness and adoption

A dedicated AI module examines a candidate’s attitude towards AI, approach to its application and ability to adopt it. This helps inform decisions when recruiting leaders responsible for AI initiatives, organisational change and measurable business outcomes.

SANTATI GROWTH DIAMOND

Capability and drive

Santati Growth Diamond provides a structured methodology for assessing employees’ Capability and Drive in relation to specific work tasks, identifying relevant development stages and the leadership support required.

The objective is to provide both the company and the candidate with a more informed basis for decision making.

Beyond placement

Our responsibility continues after the hire.

90 DAYS

Action planning

The appointed candidate prepares a 90-day action plan. The hiring company prepares a separate onboarding and expectations plan, giving both parties clear priorities for the transition.

3 · 6 · 12 MONTHS

Structured follow-up

We contact the company and candidate to review progress and identify emerging challenges.

OPTIONAL · FIRST 6 MONTHS

Coaching & mentoring

Optional coaching during the first six months for newly appointed leaders, hiring managers or employees where collaboration, performance or understanding of the role is not developing as expected. Delivered by certified Business Coaches and Career Coaches from the United Kingdom and Ireland.
